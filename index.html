01 / Natural nails

Care for the nails you have.

Gel Manicure

$35
1 hour

A classic manicure with a little extra staying power. Includes nail shaping, cuticle care, one solid gel polish color, and a relaxing hand massage.

Perfect if you love your natural nails but want a shiny, long-lasting finish.

Builder Gel Overlay

$45
1 hour 15 minutes

A layer of builder gel over your natural nails provides extra strength and support. Great for helping natural nails grow without adding length — a little suit of armor for your nails.

Soak-off removal available.

Structured Hard Gel Manicure

$50
1 hour 30 minutes

A stronger, more structured gel manicure. Builder gel creates a balanced shape and added support while keeping your nails looking natural. Ideal for nails that bend or break.

Must be filed off; not soak-off.

02 / Extensions

A little more length.

Soft Gel Extensions

$50
1 hour 30 minutes

Full-coverage soft gel tips add instant length and shape while staying lightweight and flexible. Includes prep, application, shaping, and your choice of gel polish color.

Commonly referred to as Gel-X.

Acrylic Full Set

$60
1 hour 30 minutes or more

A durable enhancement that adds length and creates a strong foundation, especially for longer nails and detailed art. Acrylic can be filled as nails grow or soaked off for a fresh set.

Maximum length: 1 inch from the cuticle. Maintenance recommended every 3 weeks.

Acrylic Fill

$45
1 hour 15 minutes or more

Refreshes new growth, shape, and balance without starting over. Very grown-out sets, significant lifting, or damage may call for removal and a new full set instead.

Maximum length: 1 inch from the cuticle.

03 / Removals

Make room for a fresh start.

Soak-Off Removal

$15
30 minutes

Removal of Just The Tips Nail Co. gel, builder gel, or soft gel extensions with care for your natural nails.

Removal only; no new service included.

Foreign Soak-Off

$20
30–45 minutes

Removal of gel, soft builder gel, soft gel extensions, or acrylic applied by another nail tech.

Price may increase if removal takes longer or the product is not soak-off.
